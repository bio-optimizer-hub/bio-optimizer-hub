Peer-Reviewed Science & Protocol FAQs

How does THCV isolate differ from traditional cannabinoids in appetite regulation?

Unlike THC, which acts as a CB1 receptor agonist stimulating hyperphagia (the 'munchies'), THCV operates as a neutral antagonist. By blocking the CB1 receptor without triggering inverse agonist side effects, it dampens the hedonic drive for food while supporting natural metabolic signaling.

Why is standard oral cannabinoid absorption inefficient?

Standard oils and edibles suffer from severe first-pass hepatic metabolism. Enzymes such as CYP2C9 and CYP3A4 degrade a vast majority of the active molecules in the liver before they ever reach systemic circulation, resulting in low bioavailability and erratic onset times.
