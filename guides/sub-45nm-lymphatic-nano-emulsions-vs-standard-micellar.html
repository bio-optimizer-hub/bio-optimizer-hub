Why is particle size below 45nm critical for cannabinoid pharmacokinetics?

Standard oral cannabinoids suffer from massive degradation in the liver due to first-pass metabolism driven by CYP2C9 and CYP3A4 enzymes. By engineering particle sizes to sub-45nm thresholds, the molecules are small enough to be packaged into chylomicrons by intestinal enterocytes and absorbed directly into the lymphatic system, raising area-under-the-curve (AUC) bioavailability to roughly 36%.

How do standard micellar solutions differ from true lymphatic nano-emulsions?

Standard micellar solutions typically yield droplet diameters between 200nm and 500nm. These larger structures are too bulky for efficient enterocyte chylomicron integration, forcing them through the portal vein where hepatic enzymes break down a significant portion of the active compounds before they ever reach systemic circulation.
