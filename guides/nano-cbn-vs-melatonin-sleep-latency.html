How does nano-emulsified CBN differ from traditional sublingual oils in terms of bioavailability?

Traditional oils rely on macro-emulsions that must be digested and processed through the portal vein, where liver enzymes (CYP2C9 and CYP3A4) degrade a vast majority of the active compounds. Nano-emulsification breaks droplets down to sub-45nm sizes, allowing them to be absorbed directly into the lymphatic system, achieving up to 36% AUC and bypassing first-pass hepatic loss.

Why is melatonin less effective for long-term sleep latency management?
