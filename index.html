Standard MCT oil tinctures require bile-salt emulsification and enter the portal vein, where hepatic CYP2C9 and CYP3A4 enzymes degrade ~90% of the dose (6–10% bioavailability). 02 // SLEEP ARCHITECTURE • NCT05511818 (N > 1,000)

Nano-CBN & Apigenin vs. Exogenous Melatonin

Randomized double-blind trials confirm 25–50 mg CBN significantly reduces nocturnal awakenings (p=0.023) without hormone-receptor desensitization. Paired with Apigenin (GABA-A positive allosteric modulator & CD38 inhibitor) and Mag L-Threonate, it elevates parasympathetic nocturnal HRV.

03 // CORTICAL FOCUS • PREFRONTAL α2-ADRENERGIC AGONISM

Cannabigerol (CBG) & Catecholamine Efficiency

Unlike caffeine, which triggers systemic sympathetic vasoconstriction, CBG acts as a nanomolar α2-adrenergic receptor agonist in the medial prefrontal cortex—enhancing pyramidal neuron firing efficiency and verbal working memory when paired with Citicoline and Lion's Mane.

THCV Neutral CB1 Antagonism & AMPK Activation

Human double-blind trials (Jadoon et al.) show Δ9-THCV acts as a neutral CB1 antagonist, blunting hypothalamic hunger signaling and lowering fasting plasma glucose. Combined with 5x-bioavailable Dihydroberberine and Tributyrin, it optimizes metabolic flexibility during fasting.
